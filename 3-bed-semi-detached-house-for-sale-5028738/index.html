About the property

Description property

DNG are delighted to present 205 Riverforest, a super 3 bedroom semi-detached property ideally located in this ever popular development close to Leixlip Village. This property boasts a low maintenance rear garden with off streed parking to the front.

205 Riverforest is perfectly positioned within a quiet cul de sac and yet is on a two minute walk from amenities such as Supervalu, while Leixlip Confey Train Station and Leixlip Village are both only a short walk.

Recently upgraded this property is presented with turn key interiors and benefits from, new upgraded kitchen, bathroom along with spacious entrance hallway.

Accommodation downstairs comprises of a wide entrance hallway, large living room with open fireplace, which opens into an openplan kitchen/diningroom. Upstairs are three well proportioned bedrooms and a recently updated and renovated bathroom.

Located within one of Leixlip's most sought after and highly regarded locations, 205 Riverforest enjoys the best of both worlds, as it is away from the hustle and bustle, while still enjoying easy access to every conceivable amenity. Ideally located just minutes walk from Leixlip village with its host of eateries, pubs and shops. Riverforest is readily accessible to Confey train station and the N4/M4/M50 are also within striking distance. This property also has the advantage of having bus services to and from Lucan, Blanchardstown & Celbridge right on its doorstep with direct access into Dublin's city centre as well by both bus and train.
